**Team of three · March–May 2023**  
Fundamentals of Electronics (ECN-102), IIT Roorkee

We designed an Arduino-controlled humanoid-to-car robot using **14 servo motors and four DC motors**, with a lithium-ion power supply. The team developed mechanical parts in SolidWorks, obtained 3D-printed components, and designed and wired the electronics.

## My contribution

I wrote most of the robot-specific C++ code, contributed to engineering design decisions, and independently managed the electronics circuitry. This included power and control circuits involving batteries, a battery management system, voltage converters, and motor actuation.

The software included abstractions for servo and DC motors, joints, wheels, car/robot control, and command handling, along with a Bluetooth/serial remote-control interface.

## Outcome

We demonstrated the functional correctness of the wired electronics. The semester ended before full assembly and integration, and the project was abandoned. **The complete robot was never built**; walking, driving, and physical transformation were not demonstrated. Some control code remained incomplete.
