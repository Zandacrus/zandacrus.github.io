I am a **Project Associate at IIT Bhubaneswar**, working with Dr. Debiprasanna Sahoo on computer architecture and simulation. My interests center on **cache and memory systems, on-chip interconnects, CPU/GPU microarchitecture, and architectures for machine learning**.

I enjoy working across the hardware–software boundary: designing RTL in SystemVerilog, building and modifying architectural simulators, and developing systems software in modern C++. My current work explores reusable interconnect interfaces for ONNXim and 3D networks-on-chip. I am also beginning an investigation of cache policies and memory behavior in NVIDIA Blackwell RTX 5000/6000 Pro GPUs.

I completed my **Integrated M.Tech. in Geophysical Technology at IIT Roorkee in June 2026**. My dissertation combined geospatial machine learning and geophysical analysis to study landslide susceptibility in the Garhwal Himalaya. Alongside my degree, research internships at IIT Delhi and IIT Bhubaneswar gave me experience in cache bandwidth management, asynchronous federated learning, and CPU/GPU workload profiling.

## Research interests

- **Memory systems:** cache organization and policies, shared LLC bandwidth, and 3D cache and memory design.
- **Interconnects and simulation:** networks-on-chip, chiplet communication, and modular simulation infrastructure.
- **Efficient compute:** CPU/GPU microarchitecture, ML/DL accelerators, and high-performance computing.
- **Performance analysis:** workload characterization, profiling, and analysis of IPC, cache misses, latency, and bandwidth.

## Selected work

**Cache bandwidth management · IIT Delhi**  
Implemented and evaluated LLC bandwidth-management policies in gem5 for a system with six CPU cores and four accelerators. [Read about this research →](/research/#cache-bandwidth-management)

**Neural-network inference on an FPGA**  
Independently built an MNIST classifier in SystemVerilog and verified it on a Basys 3 board. The design met 100 MHz timing constraints and classified 19 of 20 project test images correctly. [Explore the project →](/projects/ann-accelerator/)

**AsyncTiny**  
A C++11 asynchronous runtime for memory-constrained platforms, with coroutines, futures, tasks, timers, and cooperative scheduling. [Explore the project →](/projects/asynctiny/)
